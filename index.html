Her StoryThe girl who loves the game

Tanya is nine years old and lives in Old City, Philadelphia. She goes to General George A. McCall School, where she's a proud member of the school chess club.

She fell in love with chess early. She played her very first tournaments at age five, and she hasn't slowed down since — competing in events all across the city, testing herself against players of every age and level. When she isn't at a board in real life, you'll often find her playing online, squeezing in one more game.

Over the last couple of years she's stepped onto bigger stages: playing at the Pennsylvania state level, and this year, for the very first time, representing herself at Nationals for her age group. Every game is a chance to learn something new — and Tanya loves the learning as much as the winning.

    This SummerChess & lemonade on Elfreth's Alley

    🍋♟️

    Around the Fourth of July, Tanya set up her “Queens Supporting Queens” lemonade stand on historic Elfreth's Alley, joined by her friend Naomi — but this was no ordinary stand.

    She invited passing tourists to sit down and play a game of chess with her. It was also around the time of the FIFA World Cup in Philadelphia, so she got to meet and play with soccer fans from all across the world. Tanya happily taught the ones who were just learning how the pieces move.

    She raised $123, and gave every dollar to her school's McCall Chess Club. Her favorite part wasn't the money, though — it was meeting new people, playing, and sharing the game she loves.

    “I want to encourage more girls to play chess.”
